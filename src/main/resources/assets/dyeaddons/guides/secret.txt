How to obtain:
Secret dye rolls from secrets in the catacombs. Secrets roll as bonus loot and can be obtained when getting any secret.
Secret dye has a base 1/250,000 chance to drop.

Method:
If you only want secret dye, it is best to grind floor 7 secrets and start a new run when either the floor is cleared of secrets, or you have to backtrack a lot to get to new rooms.

If you still want to complete dungeon runs, it is best to do floor 5 because it has a short boss fight. You will want to play either berserker or tank because they clear rooms the whole run.
