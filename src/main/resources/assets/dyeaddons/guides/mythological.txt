How to obtain:
Mythological dye drops from mythological creatures. The person who spawns the mythological creature rolls the dye at full odds, anyone who gets lootshare rolls the dye at 1/5 odds like normal loot.
Mythological dye has a base chance from 1/1,000,000 to 1/10,000 depending on the rarity of the mob.
As a dye dropped from a mob, mythological dye is affected by magic find.

Method:
It is best to grind with a party, sharing all legendary and mythic creatures. It doesn't matter who kills the mob, so you can kill them fast (after everyone gets a hit).
