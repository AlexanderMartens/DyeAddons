How to obtain:
Pelt dye rolls from killing trapper animals in the mushroom desert.
Pelt dye has a base chance from 1/50,000 to 1/2,500 depending on the rarity of the animal.
As a dye dropped from a mob, pelt dye is affected by magic find.

Method:
Try to kill your trapper animal before Trevor's cooldown expires. Also bind /call trevor to a key. Make sure you don't get too close to elusive animals because they will despawn.
Try to maximize magic find and tracking. Switch to clover helmet after starting the task to not lose out on tracking.
