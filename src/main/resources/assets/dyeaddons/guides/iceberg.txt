Author: Anlg
How to Obtain:
Iceberg dye drops from any winter sea creature. The person who spawns the sea creature rolls the dye at full odds, anyone who gets lootshare rolls the dye at 1/5 odds like normal loot.
Iceberg dye's drop rate varies from 1/2,500,000 from common mobs, to 1/10,000 from mythic mobs.
As a dye dropped from a mob, iceberg dye is affected by magic find.

Method:
It is best to fish in a frozen blaze party to get as much lootshare as possible. Try hitting other people's grinches once to also get lootshare from them. For reindrakes, warp to jerry spawn and get a few hits in for lootshare, then go back to fishing.

Gear:
- 4/4 Frozen Blaze Armor w/ bobbin' time 5 or Nutcracker Armor if not with a party
- Magma Lord Necklace, Gillsplash Cloak, Bloodshot Gillsplash Belt, Gillsplash Gloves
- Giant Rod or Rod of the Sea
- Baby Yeti pet w/ Burnt Texts
