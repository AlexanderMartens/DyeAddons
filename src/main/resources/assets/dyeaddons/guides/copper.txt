How to obtain:
Copper dye has a chance to appear in the reward menu from a visitor in the garden.
The chance of copper dye depends on the rarity of the visitor, being 1/100,000 from uncommon, 1/50,000 from rare, 1/25,000 from legendary, 1/5,000 from mythic, and 1/500 from special.
Charmed visitors are more 3x likely to drop the dye. You can charm a visitor by giving them a Carrot-Colored Vinyl Set when they visit, and they will be charmed the next time they visit. You cannot give a vinyl set to a visitor that is already charmed, so at best you can charm a visitor every other time they visit.

Method:
The best way to grind copper dye is by giving Spaceman and Taylor a bachelor's rose every day and charming them every other time they visit. You should expect a copper dye about every 4 months doing this and multiple coop members can do this at the same time.
Another good passive strategy is cycling through offline visitors by warping to your garden every hour or so and checking them. This mod has an offline visitor announcement feature that sends a chat message and a title when your offline visitors are ready.
You can also give bachelor roses to legendary visitors every day to guarantee they show up next on your garden.
If you want to actively grind this dye, the best way is to farm pests and crops on your garden to cycle through visitors faster.

You can buy visitor upgrades from the desk in the garden to reduce the cooldown of visitors and increase the maximum number of visitors that can visit your garden at a time.
The visitor bait attribute (mudworm shard) and queenly echo attribute (queen snake shard) reduce the cooldown of visitors by up to 17.5%.
The fancy visitor attribute (invisibug shard), Finnegan's blooming business perk, and the copper talisman line makes rare and higher visitors more likely to visit.
