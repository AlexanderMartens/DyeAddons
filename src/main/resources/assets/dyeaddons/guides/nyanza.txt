How to obtain:
Nyanza dye has a chance to appear as a reward for completing a commission.
Nyanza dye has a base 1/100,000 chance to appear.

Method:
The fastest commissions to do is in the glacite tunnels. In the glacite tunnels, you can fit 2 commissions into every pickobulus cooldown. The best place to do commissions is around the great glacite lake.
For best rates, leave every mineshaft instantly, but you should mine fossil blocks if you also want fossil dye and open corpses if you also want frostbitten dye.
