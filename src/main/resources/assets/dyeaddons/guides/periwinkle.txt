Author: Anlg
How to obtain:
Periwinkle dye rolls from killing runic mobs. You can identify runic mobs by their purple name tag and pink health. They also have 4x hp and do 2x damage.
Periwinkle dye has a base chance of 1/400,000 to 1/25,000 depending on the level of the mob.
As a dye dropped from a mob, periwinkle dye is affected by magic find.

This dye is best done passively by killing runic mobs whenever you see one. This mod has a feature that makes runic mob's nametags bigger, which make them easier to find at large distances and through walls.

Method 1: Smoldering Blaze
Smoldering blazes barely make the highest tier of periwinkle dye, and you can kill a lot of them. You can either lobby hop and look for runic mobs or stay in a lobby and kill smoldering blazes for a chance of also getting cyclamen dye.

Method 2: Kill high level mobs in dungeons
Most dungeon mobs in F7 and up are over level 200. If you do f7, you can also enter the boss room and kill the wither guards/miners for more potential runic mobs. During clear, you can go to the middle of the room and look around for purple nametags for runic mobs.
