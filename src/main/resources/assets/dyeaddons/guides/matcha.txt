How to obtain:
Matcha dye rolls from the Revenant Horror slayer boss.
Matcha dye's base chance depends on the tier of the boss. At tier 5, the dye has a base 1/100,000 chance of dropping and is affected by magic find.
There is a rng meter for this dye to guarantee a dye after 25,000,000 progress.

Method:
The fastest way to get the dye is from killing tier 5 bosses in the crypt in the hub.
