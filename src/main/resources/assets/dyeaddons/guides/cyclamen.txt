How to obtain:
Cyclamen dye rolls when you kill some mobs on the crimson isle. Everyone that damages crimson isle minibosses roll for the dye.
Cyclamen dye has a base chance ranging from 1/10,000,000 to 1/250,000.
As a dye dropped from a mob, cyclamen dye is affected by magic find. Crimson isle minibosses are not affected by magic find.

Method:
The fastest way to grind this dye is by killing hellwisps because they have a base 1/250,000 chance of dropping the dye and are affected by magic find.
Hellwisps can be found in the cave near the Matriarch. There are four lava pools they spawn in, and every 15 seconds, two attempt to spawn in random lava pools.
It is recommended to kill hellwisps with either a hyperion or flaming flay. Make sure you are holding a weapon with looting when the hellwisp dies.
You can use a black hole on hellwisps and still roll for the dye for extra profit. Make sure you have your looting weapon out when you get the shards as that's when the dye rolls.

Gear:
- Hyperion or flaming flay w/ looting 7
- Clover Helmet
