How to obtain:
Byzantium dye rolls from the Voidgloom Seraph slayer boss.
Byzantium dye's base chance depends on the tier of the boss. At tier 4, the dye has a base 1/250,000 chance of dropping and is affected by magic find.
There is a rng meter for this dye to guarantee a dye after 25,000,000 progress.

Method:
The fastest way to get the dye is from killing tier 4 bosses in the void sepulture in the end.
