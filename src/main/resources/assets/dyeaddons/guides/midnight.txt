How to obtain:
Midnight dye drops from spooky mobs and spooky sea creatures. Everyone that does damage to headless horseman rolls for the dye at full odds.
Midnight dye's drop rate varies from 1/1,000,000 from common, uncommon, and rare spooky sea creatures, to 1/10,000 from mythic sea creatures, headless horseman, and bat piñatas.
As a dye dropped from a mob, midnight dye is affected by magic find.

Method 1: Headless Horseman
You can spawn headless horseman in the wilderness or graveyard in the hub at night or during the spooky festival.
Spawn the horseman in an enclosed area and kill it with an aspect of the dragon's ability because it has no cooldown. You can have multiple people spawn and kill it at the same time for better rates.

Method 2: Spooky fishing
During spooky festival you can fish sea creatures for the dye.
Make sure you are using the spooky hook, spooky bait, and bat pet for the most spooky sea creatures.
It is best to fish in backwater bayou for bobbin time buff or crystal hollows for miner's blessing perk.
Use frozen blaze with a party to get lootshare and 2x rates.
