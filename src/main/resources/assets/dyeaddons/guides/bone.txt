Author: Anlg
How to obtain:
Bone dye rolls every time you kill a skeletal mob. This includes all mobs with the skeletal mob type, as well as skeleton spawned from minions.
Bone dye has a base 1/3,000,000 chance to drop from skeletal mobs.
As a dye dropped from a mob, bone dye is affected by magic find.

Method:
The best way to get this dye is to kill skeletons spawned from minions on your private island. You can funnel mobs spawned from minions with water into a small chamber to kill. You can use a weapon with aoe ability to kill all the skeletons at once. Make sure you are holding a weapon with looting when the skeletons die.
Note: Afking with toggle break/use item is against hypixel rules.

Gear:
- Any aoe damage weapon with looting 7
- Clover Helmet
