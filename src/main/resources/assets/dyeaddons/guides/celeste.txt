How to obtain:
Celeste dye rolls from the Sven Packmaster slayer boss.
Celeste dye's base chance depends on the tier of the boss. At tier 4, the dye has a base 1/250,000 chance of dropping.
Like most slayers, celeste dye is affected by magic find.
There is a rng meter for this dye to guarantee a dye after 25,000,000 progress.

Method:
The fastest way to get the dye is from killing tier 4 bosses in the cave in the jungle or acacia island in the park.
